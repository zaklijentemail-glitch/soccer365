**The best American soccer player of all time is Landon Donovan, who shares the national scoring record of 57 goals with Clint Dempsey and scored five World Cup goals, while the best American playing right now is Christian Pulisic.** Below are the ten best American men's players ever, then the seven who matter most after the 2026 World Cup.

## How the list is ranked

The all-time list ranks on documented record, in this order: what a player did for the United States (caps, goals, World Cups, tournament honours), what he did at club level and where (the bigger the league and the trophy, the more weight), then individual recognition such as All-Star teams and the National Soccer Hall of Fame. Older eras count on the same terms, but only on a record that can be checked.

The right-now list is ranked on the 2025-26 club season, the last one completed, and on the 2026 World Cup. Caps and goals are as of mid-November 2026; where sources disagreed on a number, it is left out. This page covers the men's national team only.

## 1. Landon Donovan (LA Galaxy, United States)

**Career:** 157 caps, 57 goals, 58 assists. **Honours:** six MLS Cups, FIFA World Cup Best Young Player 2002.

Donovan's numbers are the benchmark. His 57 goals tie him with Dempsey at the top of the scoring list, and his 58 international assists were the highest total in international football [until 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landon_Donovan). He scored five World Cup goals, the most by an American man, and at the 2002 tournament he won FIFA's Best Young Player award, still the only CONCACAF player to do so.

The goal everyone remembers came in 2010: a late winner against Algeria that put the USA top of its World Cup group for the first time since 1930. At club level he won a record six MLS Cups, two with San Jose and four with LA Galaxy, and had two loan spells at Everton. He was named U.S. Soccer Player of the Year four times, a record he now shares with Pulisic, and entered the National Soccer Hall of Fame in 2023.

## 2. Clint Dempsey (Fulham, United States)

**Career:** 141 caps, 57 goals. **Honours:** MLS Cup 2016, Supporters' Shield 2014, U.S. Open Cup 2014.

Dempsey is the only American to have scored at three World Cups, in 2006, 2010 and 2014, and his goal against Ghana in 2014, about 30 seconds in, is the fastest the USA has scored at the tournament, according to [U.S. Soccer](https://www.ussoccer.com/stories/2026/06/usmnt/usmnt-enjoys-historic-group-stage-performance-fifa-world-cup-2026).

His 50 Premier League goals for Fulham are the club's record in the competition, and on 21 January 2012 he scored the first hat-trick by an American in the competition, in a 5-2 win over Newcastle. His chip against Juventus in 2010 sent Fulham on towards the Europa League final. He won the 2016 MLS Cup with Seattle and was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2022.

## 3. Christian Pulisic (AC Milan, United States)

**Career:** 91 caps, 35 goals. **Honours:** Champions League 2021, Supercoppa Italiana 2024-25, three CONCACAF Nations Leagues.

Pulisic is the only active player near the top of the records: fourth on the scoring list, behind Dempsey, Donovan and Jozy Altidore (42), [per NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/news/usmnt-records-all-time-scoring-leaders-cap-winners-coaches). In 2021 he became the first American to play in a Champions League final, and only the second to win one, when Chelsea beat Manchester City 1-0.

At Milan he has been one of Serie A's best attackers, named in the league's Team of the Season in 2023-24 and 2024-25. He has won four U.S. Soccer Player of the Year awards, level with Donovan.

## 4. Tim Howard (Everton, United States)

**Career:** 121 caps, the most by any US goalkeeper. **Honours:** FA Cup 2004, PFA Team of the Year 2003-04.

Howard won the FA Cup and a place in the PFA Team of the Year in his first season at Manchester United, then made 414 appearances for Everton, where he set the club record for league clean sheets in a season in 2008-09. In the 2014 round of 16 against Belgium he made more saves than any goalkeeper in a World Cup match since records began in 1966; [MLS counted 16](https://www.mlssoccer.com/post/2014/07/01/world-cup-usmnt-goalkeeper-tim-howard-sets-world-cup-record-16-saves-vs-belgium) on the night, and the USA still lost 2-1 after extra time. He entered the Hall of Fame in 2024.

## 5. Claudio Reyna (Rangers, United States)

**Career:** 112 caps, 8 goals. **Honours:** Scottish Premier League and Scottish Cup 1999-2000.

Reyna captained the USA from 1998 to 2007, including at the 2002 and 2006 World Cups. In 2002, when the USA reached the quarter-finals, he became the first American named in a World Cup All-Star team. He played for Bayer Leverkusen, became the first American to captain a European club at Wolfsburg, won the double with Rangers, then played in the Premier League for Sunderland and Manchester City. He was elected to the Hall of Fame in 2012.

## 6. Brad Friedel (Blackburn Rovers, United States)

**Career:** 450 Premier League appearances. **Honours:** League Cup 2002.

Friedel holds the Premier League record of 310 consecutive appearances, which ended in October 2012, and his 450 league games are the most by any player from the Americas. He was man of the match when Blackburn beat Tottenham in the 2002 League Cup final, and at the 2002 World Cup he became only the second goalkeeper, after Jan Tomaszewski, to save two penalties outside a shootout at a finals. He later became Tottenham's oldest player, and entered the Hall of Fame in 2018. His cap total is left out because sources disagree.

## 7. Michael Bradley (Toronto FC, United States)

**Career:** 151 caps, 17 goals. **Honours:** MLS Cup 2017 and the domestic treble.

Bradley is third on the all-time caps list and started every US game at the 2010 and 2014 World Cups. He played in the Netherlands and Italy, including two seasons at Roma, before returning to captain Toronto FC to their first MLS Cup in 2017, a 2-0 win over Seattle that completed a domestic treble. He retired in 2023 and in December 2025 became head coach of the New York Red Bulls. The demands of the role he played are covered in [box-to-box midfielder explained](/guides/box-to-box-midfielder-explained).

## 8. Brian McBride (Fulham, United States)

**Career:** 96 caps, 30 goals. **Honours:** Fulham captain 2007-08.

McBride was the first American to score at two World Cups, with three goals across 1998 and 2002. He scored 32 Premier League goals in 140 league games for Fulham and captained the club; in 2009 Fulham renamed a bar at Craven Cottage "McBride's" in his honour. Before England he scored 62 league goals for Columbus Crew, who made him the first member of their Circle of Honor. He entered the Hall of Fame in 2014.

## 9. Kasey Keller (Tottenham Hotspur, United States)

**Career:** 102 caps, a US record 47 clean sheets and 53 wins. **Honours:** League Cup 1997.

Keller was in four World Cup squads, from 1990 to 2006, and started all three games in 2006. He won the 1997 League Cup with Leicester, then kept goal in Spain for Rayo Vallecano, in the Premier League for Tottenham and in the Bundesliga for Borussia Mönchengladbach. He was U.S. Soccer Athlete of the Year in 1997, 1999 and 2005, and entered the Hall of Fame in 2015.

## 10. DaMarcus Beasley (PSV Eindhoven, United States)

**Career:** 126 caps, 17 goals. **Honours:** two Eredivisie titles, two Scottish titles, four Gold Cups.

Beasley is the only American man to play at four World Cups: 2002, 2006, 2010 and 2014. In 2005 he became the first American to play in a Champions League semi-final, scoring four times in PSV's run. He won two league titles in the Netherlands and two with Rangers, captained the USA to the 2013 Gold Cup and entered the Hall of Fame in 2023.

## Just missing out

**Cobi Jones** has more caps than anyone, 164, but less of the club and tournament record that separates the ten above. **Jozy Altidore** is third on the scoring list with 42. **Bert Patenaude** belongs in any history of the national team: at the 1930 World Cup, where the USA reached the semi-finals, he scored more than once in a match, something no other American did until Folarin Balogun in 2026.

## The best American players right now

At the 2026 World Cup the hosts won their group, beat Bosnia and Herzegovina 2-0 for a first knockout win since 2002, then [lost 4-1 to Belgium](https://ussoccer.com/stories/2026/07/usmnt/match-recap-highlights-vs-belgium-world-cup-round-of-16) in the round of 16. ### 1. Christian Pulisic (AC Milan)

Eight Serie A goals in 2025-26 and the best record of any active American. Injury in the opening win over Paraguay cost him the Australia game, but no current American comes close to his body of work.

### 2. Folarin Balogun (Monaco)

The breakout of 2026. Thirteen Ligue 1 goals, then two in his World Cup debut against Paraguay and another against Bosnia and Herzegovina. U.S. Soccer notes that only Bert Patenaude, in 1930, had previously scored more than once in a World Cup match for the USA.

### 3. Malik Tillman (Bayer Leverkusen)

Tillman scored direct free kicks in both knockout games, the second player since 1966 to score two in one World Cup and the first American to score in consecutive World Cup knockout matches. At club level he added two Champions League goals for Leverkusen.

### 4. Weston McKennie (Juventus)

McKennie had 17 goal contributions in 48 games for Juventus in 2025-26, and he captained the USA for the fourth time against Türkiye at the World Cup.

### 5. Chris Richards (Crystal Palace)

A Conference League winner in 2025-26 a year after the FA Cup, and a World Cup starter at centre-back who completed every one of his passes against Paraguay.

### 6. Antonee Robinson (Fulham)

The 2024 U.S. Soccer Player of the Year made 10 Premier League assists in 2024-25, the most by any defender in the league. A lighter 2025-26 drops him below Richards.

### 7. Tyler Adams (Bournemouth)

Adams was the youngest captain at the 2022 World Cup and started against Belgium in 2026.
