**The best centre-back in the world in 2026 is Barcelona's Pau Cubarsí**, who won La Liga at 19 and then played every minute of Spain's World Cup win, with Arsenal's Gabriel Magalhães and William Saliba next. Below is the full top ten, with a stat line for each player from the 2025-26 season.

## How the list is ranked

Three things, in this order:

1. **The 2025-26 club season.** League appearances as a measure of availability, the trophies the player's team won, and the end-of-season selections that judge individual defenders: the PFA Team of the Year, UEFA's Champions League Team of the Season and the UNFP Ligue 1 Team of the Year.
2. **The 2026 World Cup.** A tournament in the summer of 2026 is the most recent evidence there is, so it counts. Players whose countries did not qualify are not marked down for that alone.
3. **The team's defensive record**, where it helps separate two close cases.

Reputation and transfer value are not criteria. Stat lines are for 2025-26 and give league appearances and goals, then all competitions.

## 1. Pau Cubarsí (Barcelona, Spain)

**2025-26:** 31 La Liga appearances, 1 goal; 48 appearances, 1 goal in all competitions.

Cubarsí's season would have earned him a place on this list on club form alone. Barcelona won La Liga for the second year running, clinching it with three games to spare by beating Real Madrid 2-0 on 10 May, and conceded 36 league goals, the fewest in Spain. They also won the Supercopa.

The World Cup is what puts him first. He [played every minute of Spain's run](https://theanalyst.com/articles/pau-cubarsi-world-cup-2026-young-player-of-the-tournament), Spain conceded once in eight matches and kept seven clean sheets, and he finished with FIFA's Young Player Award. He was not a passenger in possession either: Opta's figures credit him with 671 successful passes at the tournament, the second-most by any player at a World Cup since 1966, and 221 forward passes, the most of anyone. In the final against Argentina, which Spain won 1-0 after extra time, he had 139 touches, more than any other player on the pitch.

At 19 years and 178 days, he was the fourth-youngest player to appear in a World Cup final.

## 2. Gabriel Magalhães (Arsenal, Brazil)

**2025-26:** 32 Premier League appearances, 3 goals; 51 appearances, 4 goals in all competitions.

Arsenal won the Premier League with 85 points, their first title since the 2003-04 Invincibles, and conceded 27 league goals, the fewest in the division. Gabriel was at the centre of it and was picked in both the [PFA Team of the Year](https://www.arsenal.com/news/five-gunners-named-in-pfa-team-of-the-year-axXNQ7K6Jpdf) and [UEFA's Champions League Team of the Season](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a5-20c1d7d33d69-be4f6ab11b0a-1000--2025-26-uefa-champions-league-team-of-the-season/), the only centre-back to make both.

He adds goals as well as clean sheets: four in all competitions, three of them in the league. The season ended badly, though. Arsenal lost the Champions League final to PSG on penalties, and Gabriel was one of the two Arsenal players who missed in the shootout.

## 3. William Saliba (Arsenal, France)

**2025-26:** 31 Premier League appearances, 1 goal; 50 appearances, 1 goal in all competitions.

Saliba was Gabriel's partner for most of the title run, and both made the PFA Team of the Year. Gabriel edges it on the Champions League selection and his goals. Saliba also signed a new contract in September 2025 running to 2030.

His summer ended early. He was substituted in the first half of France's World Cup semi-final against Spain with a back injury, France lost 2-0, and [Arsenal confirmed](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/7/23/william-saliba-back-injury-arsenal-france-world-cup) he would be out for "an extended period" but that surgery was not recommended. That absence affects his 2026-27 season, not the ranking here.

## 4. Willian Pacho (PSG, Ecuador)

**2025-26:** 23 Ligue 1 appearances, 0 goals; 44 appearances, 2 goals in all competitions.

Pacho won almost everything there was to win. PSG took a fifth consecutive Ligue 1 title, finishing six points clear of Lens, and won the Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup, the Trophée des Champions and the Intercontinental Cup. He started the Champions League final and was named in the [UNFP Ligue 1 Team of the Year](https://www.psg.fr/en/content/paris-saint-germain-shine-at-unfp-awards-2026-psg-news), one of five PSG players in it.

His 17 European appearances were the most of anyone on this list. He was also in Ecuador's squad for the World Cup.

## 5. Marquinhos (PSG, Brazil)

**2025-26:** 14 Ligue 1 appearances, 0 goals; 32 appearances, 2 goals in all competitions.

Marquinhos made fewer league appearances than anyone in the top ten, which is why he is not higher; a [left thigh injury](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250926-psg-captain-marquinhos-out-with-thigh-injury) in late September 2025 kept him out for several weeks. In the Champions League, where PSG's season was decided, he made 15 appearances and scored twice. He captained the club to its second Champions League title and was picked in UEFA's Team of the Season alongside Gabriel.

He has been PSG captain since September 2020 and was in Brazil's 26-man squad for the World Cup.

## 6. Dayot Upamecano (Bayern Munich, France)

**2025-26:** 24 Bundesliga appearances, 1 goal; 42 appearances, 2 goals in all competitions.

Bayern won the Bundesliga with four games to spare and 89 points, then completed the double by beating VfB Stuttgart 3-0 in the DFB-Pokal final on 23 May, with Upamecano starting at centre-back. In February 2026 he extended his contract with the club to 2030.

France, his national team, reached the World Cup semi-finals and finished fourth after losing the third-place match 6-4 to England.

## 7. Virgil van Dijk (Liverpool, Netherlands)

**2025-26:** 38 Premier League appearances, 6 goals; 55 appearances, 8 goals in all competitions.

On individual numbers Van Dijk had as strong a season as anyone here. He played every league game, and his eight goals in all competitions are the most on this list. What keeps him at seven is the team around him: Liverpool finished fifth with 53 goals conceded and won nothing, and head coach Arne Slot left at the end of May.

The World Cup did not change things. The Netherlands went out in the round of 32, [beaten 3-2 on penalties by Morocco](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/6/30/morocco-netherlands-world-cup-knockout-penalty-shootout-saibari-diop-gakpo) after a 1-1 draw. He turned 35 in July.

## 8. Alessandro Bastoni (Inter, Italy)

**2025-26:** 28 Serie A appearances, 1 goal; 40 appearances, 2 goals in all competitions.

Inter won Serie A with 87 points, 11 clear of Napoli, clinching it with three games to spare. Bastoni was not, though, the league's best defender in Lega Serie A's own end-of-season awards, which went to Cagliari's Marco Palestra.

The low point came for Italy. In the World Cup play-off final against Bosnia and Herzegovina on 31 March, Bastoni was [sent off in the 41st minute](https://football-italia.net/bosnia-1-1-italy-4-1-on-pens-fail-world-cup/) for a foul on Amar Memić as he ran through on goal. Ten-man Italy drew 1-1 and lost the shootout 4-1, missing a third consecutive World Cup.

## 9. Aymeric Laporte (Athletic Club, Spain)

**2025-26:** 25 La Liga appearances, 0 goals; 30 appearances, 0 goals in all competitions.

Laporte's club season at Athletic Club would not earn him a top-ten place by itself. His summer does. He started all eight of Spain's World Cup matches next to Cubarsí, and the two of them were the centre of a defence that conceded once on the way to the title. In the final he played the full 120 minutes.

## 10. Marc Guéhi (Crystal Palace and Manchester City, England)

**2025-26:** 35 Premier League appearances, 3 goals (20 apps and 2 goals for Palace, 15 apps and 1 goal for City).

Guéhi started the season as Crystal Palace captain and finished it at Manchester City, who [signed him on 19 January 2026](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4549796/man-city-sign-marc-guehi-from-crystal-palace-in-january-transfer-window) on a five-and-a-half-year contract. City finished second.

At the World Cup he came off the bench in England's opening game and became a regular starter alongside Ezri Konsa through the knockout rounds, as England finished third.

## Just missing out

**Dean Huijsen** (Real Madrid) played 28 La Liga games and scored twice, but was left out of the Spain squad that won the World Cup. **Micky van de Ven** (Tottenham) scored seven goals in all competitions, but Spurs finished 17th and only secured safety on the final day.

## Where the best centre-backs play

Six of the top ten spent 2025-26 in the Premier League or at PSG, and the two pairs near the top have something in common: they played as partnerships all season. Gabriel and Saliba for Arsenal, and Pacho and Marquinhos for PSG, started the Champions League final against each other.
