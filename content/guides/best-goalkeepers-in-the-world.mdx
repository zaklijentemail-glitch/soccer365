**The best goalkeeper in the world in 2026 is Spain's Unai Simón**, who kept a record seven clean sheets as Spain won the World Cup and then won the Yashin Trophy, with Arsenal's David Raya and Barcelona's Joan García next. Below is the full top ten, with a stat line for each from the 2025-26 season.

## How the list is ranked

Three things, in this order:

1. **The 2025-26 club season.** League appearances and clean sheets, the trophies the team won, and the awards that judge goalkeepers directly: the Premier League Golden Glove, Spain's Zamora Trophy and the PFA Team of the Year.
2. **The 2026 World Cup.** It ended on 19 July 2026 and is the most recent evidence there is. Keepers whose countries did not qualify are not marked down for that alone.
3. **The 2026 Yashin Trophy vote**, announced on 26 October 2026 and covering 3 August 2025 to 19 July 2026, as a check on the first two.

Reputation and transfer value are not criteria. Stat lines are league games and clean sheets in 2025-26, from the club's domestic league.

## 1. Unai Simón (Athletic Club, Spain)

**2025-26:** 37 La Liga appearances, 6 clean sheets. **World Cup:** 8 matches, 7 clean sheets, 1 goal conceded.

On club form alone Simón would not be on this list. Athletic Club finished 12th and conceded 58 league goals, and [The Analyst](https://theanalyst.com/players/1231/unai-simon) credits him with six clean sheets.

The World Cup changed everything. Luis de la Fuente chose him ahead of Raya and García, and he played all eight of Spain's matches. The only goal he conceded was Charles De Ketelaere's in the 41st minute of the 2-1 quarter-final win over Belgium; the other seven games were clean sheets, the most by a goalkeeper at a single World Cup, and [Fox Sports](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/soccer/spain-goalkeeper-unai-simon-wins-golden-glove-after-historic-world-cup) reported that Spain conceded the fewest goals any team has on the way to winning the tournament. Along the way he passed Walter Zenga's 517-minute World Cup run without conceding, which had stood since 1990. Spain beat Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the final, Ferran Torres scoring in the 106th minute, and Simón won the Golden Glove.

On 26 October he [won the Yashin Trophy](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/343937/unai-simon-wins-2026-yashin-trophy-moroccos-bounou-finishes-eighth/) at the London Palladium, two years after finishing second to Martínez.

## 2. David Raya (Arsenal, Spain)

**2025-26:** 37 Premier League appearances, 19 clean sheets.

Raya had the best club season of any goalkeeper here. His 19 clean sheets won him [a third straight Golden Glove](https://www.arsenal.com/news/david-raya-completes-golden-glove-hat-trick-aitsS9X1ZqjV), four more than Donnarumma, and only Pepe Reina, Joe Hart and Ederson had won three in a row before him. One more would equal the record of four held by Petr Čech and Hart. Arsenal won the title with 85 points and conceded 27 goals, and he was the goalkeeper in the PFA Team of the Year.

In the Champions League final against PSG in Budapest on 30 May he [saved Nuno Mendes' penalty](https://www.nbcmiami.com/news/sports/psg-wins-champions-league-shootout-victory-arsenal/) in the shootout, but Eberechi Eze and Gabriel missed and PSG won 4-3.

He was in Spain's World Cup-winning squad as a back-up and did not play, which is the one thing separating him from Simón.

## 3. Joan García (Barcelona, Spain)

**2025-26:** 30 La Liga appearances, 15 clean sheets.

Barcelona paid Espanyol his €25 million release clause in June 2025, and he repaid it in one season. Barcelona won La Liga with 94 points, clinching it with a 2-0 win over Real Madrid on 10 May, and won the Supercopa. García conceded [21 goals in 30 league games](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/4508895/joan-garcia-20252026-laliga-zamora-trophy-winner), an average of 0.7 a game, to win the Zamora Trophy ahead of Courtois.

He ranks above Courtois on that head-to-head and on the title. Like Raya, he was a back-up for Spain at the World Cup, which kept him down at seventh in the Yashin vote.

## 4. Thibaut Courtois (Real Madrid, Belgium)

**2025-26:** 32 La Liga appearances, 13 clean sheets.

Courtois was runner-up for the Zamora and kept 13 league clean sheets in 32 games, but Real Madrid won nothing. They finished second, lost the Supercopa final to Barcelona and went out of the Champions League to Bayern Munich in the quarter-finals, which he missed with a thigh injury.

At the World Cup he took Belgium to the quarter-finals, then went off injured with the score 1-1 against Spain. His replacement, Senne Lammens, could not hold Pau Cubarsí's shot and Mikel Merino scored the winner. He still finished third in the Yashin vote.

## 5. Matvey Safonov (PSG, Russia)

**2025-26:** 15 Ligue 1 appearances; 11 Champions League appearances.

Safonov began the season as Lucas Chevalier's back-up and ended it as a Champions League winner. In the Intercontinental Cup final against Flamengo in Doha on 17 December, he made [four successive saves](https://www.foxsports.com/articles/soccer/safonov-makes-4-penalty-saves-in-shootout-as-psg-beats-flamengo-to-win-intercontinental-cup) in the shootout, from Saúl Ñíguez, Pedro, Léo Pereira and Luiz Araújo, as PSG won it 2-1. He later turned out to have fractured his hand in that game.

Luis Enrique dropped Chevalier after a run of mistakes and picked Safonov for the Champions League game against Newcastle on 28 January, and Safonov started the final against Arsenal. PSG also won a fifth consecutive Ligue 1 title. With only 15 league games, though, his season was shorter than anyone above him.

## 6. Emiliano Martínez (Aston Villa, Argentina)

**2025-26:** 32 Premier League appearances, 7 clean sheets.

His league numbers were modest, but Martínez finished his last season at Villa with a trophy. He broke a finger in the warm-up before the Europa League final against Freiburg in Istanbul on 20 May, then [kept a clean sheet in a 3-0 win](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12040/13546486/aston-villa-win-europa-league-inside-unai-emerys-latest-european-triumph-including-emiliano-martinezs-broken-finger-and-beers-with-prince-william). Villa finished fourth.

At the World Cup he started all eight of Argentina's matches and kept two clean sheets. In the final he made 11 saves, the most by a goalkeeper in a World Cup final, and still lost. He joined Chelsea on 30 August 2026 for £7.5 million, and finished fifth in the Yashin vote.

## 7. Gregor Kobel (Borussia Dortmund, Switzerland)

**2025-26:** 34 Bundesliga appearances, 15 clean sheets.

Kobel played every league game and [kept 15 clean sheets](https://theanalyst.com/football/player/sc-185513/gregor-kobel) as Dortmund finished second with 73 points. At the World Cup he helped Switzerland top their group, then saved Cucho Hernández's penalty in a 4-3 shootout win over Colombia that took them to their first World Cup quarter-final since 1954. Argentina beat them 3-1 after extra time.

## 8. Gianluigi Donnarumma (Manchester City, Italy)

**2025-26:** 34 Premier League appearances, 15 clean sheets.

The 2025 Yashin winner [joined City from PSG](https://www.ewn.co.za/2025/09/02/man-city-sign-goalkeeper-donnarumma-from-psg-as-ederson-departs) on 2 September 2025 for a reported £26 million, and was second to Raya in the Golden Glove race as City finished second. James Trafford played the League Cup final, which City won 2-0 against Arsenal.

The low point came in March. Italy drew 1-1 with Bosnia and Herzegovina in the World Cup play-off final in Zenica despite [several saves from Donnarumma](https://www.uefa.com/european-qualifiers/news/02a3-2047c06e29c6-0a8f1c84272a-1000--bosnia-and-herzegovina-1-1-italy-4-3-on-pens-hosts-win-2026), then lost the shootout. He was not on the Yashin shortlist.

## 9. Yassine Bounou (Al Hilal, Morocco)

**2025-26:** 26 Saudi Pro League appearances, 14 clean sheets.

Bounou conceded 20 goals in 26 league games, and his World Cup gave him the moment of the summer. Against the Netherlands in the round of 32 in Monterrey, after a 1-1 draw, he [saved the decisive penalty from Summerville](https://www.cafonline.com/fifa-world-cup/news/morocco-stun-netherlands-on-penalties-to-reach-world-cup-last-16/) and Morocco won the shootout 3-2. They beat Canada 3-0 before France knocked them out 2-0 in the quarter-finals. He finished eighth in the Yashin vote.

## 10. Jordan Pickford (Everton, England)

**2025-26:** 38 Premier League appearances, 11 clean sheets.

Pickford played every league game for an Everton side that finished 13th, and his 11 clean sheets were joint third in the division. At the World Cup he started the quarter-final win over Norway, his [18th World Cup appearance for England](https://www.englandfootball.com/articles/2026/Jul/11/jordan-pickford-sets-new-england-world-cup-apprearance-record-20261107), breaking Peter Shilton's record of 17. England lost the semi-final 2-1 to Argentina; Dean Henderson played the third-place win over France.

## Just missing out

**Manuel Neuer** (Bayern Munich), who turned 40 in March, played 22 league games as Bayern won the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal double, and [performed heroics](https://www.rte.ie/sport/soccer/2026/0506/1571919-psg-thwart-bayern-set-up-final-showdown-with-arsenal/) in the 1-1 Champions League semi-final second leg against PSG, who went through 6-5 on aggregate. **Vozinha**, Cape Verde's 40-year-old, finished sixth in the Yashin vote after a 0-0 draw with Spain and [four big saves against Lionel Messi](https://www.ksl.com/article/51593663/magical-world-cup-ride-of-vozinha-and-cape-verde-ends-after-pushing-messi-and-argentina-to-the-brink) in a 3-2 extra-time defeat by Argentina in the round of 32. **Édouard Mendy** captained Al Ahli to a second straight AFC Champions League Elite title.
