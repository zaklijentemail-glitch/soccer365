**Hamit Altıntop is a retired Turkey international midfielder who spent four seasons at Bayern Munich from 2007 to 2011, winning the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal double twice and starting the 2010 Champions League final.** Born in Gelsenkirchen on 8 December 1982, he also played for Schalke, Real Madrid and Galatasaray, won 82 caps for Turkey, was named in UEFA's Euro 2008 Team of the Tournament and won FIFA's Puskás Award for the 2010 goal of the year.

His career is often reduced to one volley in Astana. The Bayern years say more about the player: a versatile midfielder who could play on either flank and at right-back, used by three very different coaches in four seasons.

## Gelsenkirchen and Schalke

Hamit and Halil Altıntop, identical twins, came through local clubs in Gelsenkirchen before moving together to Wattenscheid 09, then in the third tier, in 1997. Halil made his professional debut first, in early 2001; Hamit followed a week later against Sachsen Leipzig. According to the [German FA's data centre](https://datencenter.dfb.de/en/data-center/people/hamit-altintop/player), Hamit played 64 matches for Wattenscheid between 2000 and 2003.

He joined Schalke in summer 2003 and, as [These Football Times](https://thesefootballtimes.co/2021/02/15/the-brothers-altintop-identical-faces-contrasting-paths/) recounts, scored both goals on his Schalke debut, a 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund. He made his Turkey debut against Denmark in February 2004.

Over four seasons he made 148 appearances for Schalke, 113 of them in the Bundesliga. He won the 2005 Ligapokal, played in a side that reached a UEFA Cup semi-final before losing to Sevilla, and was joined by Halil in summer 2006. That 2006-07 season ended in heartbreak: Schalke led the table for much of it, lost to Dortmund on the penultimate weekend and finished [two points behind Stuttgart](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/hakan-calhanoglu-nuri-sahn-altintop-basturk-top-5-turkish-players-25343).

By then his own departure was settled. He turned down a new contract, saying he had not been given regular football under Mirko Slomka despite performing well. In February 2007, [UEFA reported](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01be-0e6e27c0051a-7032bf552901-1000--altintop-agrees-to-bayern-move), he had appeared in 19 of Schalke's 20 league games that season but started only eight.

## The Bayern years, 2007-2011

On 14 February 2007 Schalke confirmed that Altıntop would leave for Bayern Munich on a free transfer at the end of the season. "I have decided to accept Bayern's three-year offer," he said, adding that he wanted to leave Schalke with the league title. Ottmar Hitzfeld, Bayern's coach, explained the signing in terms that would describe Altıntop's whole time in Munich: "He is a team player, he has good stamina and can play in defensive or attacking roles."

To [Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/2405602/altintop-to-join-bayern) he set out a bigger ambition: "I want to become a leading player at Bayern."

In all competitions he played 109 times for Bayern, including 17 Champions League games and nine in the UEFA Cup.

**2007-08: the double at the first attempt.** Under Hitzfeld he played regularly and Bayern won the league and cup [in his debut season](https://www.beinsports.com/en/uefa-champions-league/news/where-are-they-now-hamit-altentop/1449280). With 23 league appearances, it was his most regular season in Munich.

**2008-09: the Klinsmann season.** Hitzfeld left and Jürgen Klinsmann's stop-start season ended with his dismissal in April after a 5-1 defeat at Wolfsburg. Altıntop made only 11 league appearances.

**2009-10: van Gaal, the double and Madrid.** Louis van Gaal's first season brought a second double and a run to the Champions League final. Altıntop had not been a regular in the league, but van Gaal picked him for the biggest game of all. On 22 May 2010 at the Bernabéu he started for Bayern against Inter, with Philipp Lahm, Bastian Schweinsteiger, Mark van Bommel, Thomas Müller, Ivica Olić and Arjen Robben around him. Diego Milito scored in the 35th minute; Altıntop was replaced by Miroslav Klose in the 63rd; Milito scored again in the 70th, and Inter won 2-0 ([DFB match data](https://datencenter.dfb.de/en/data-center/champions-league/2009-2010/final/bayern-munich-inter-milan-107236)). He later called the night "bittersweet for me, but at the end of the day it was a great experience."

**2010-11: the last season.** On 2 June 2010 Bayern [extended his contract](https://www.foxsports.com/stories/soccer/bayern-extends-altintops-contract) by one more season. It was hampered by injury; Bayern finished third, won only the Supercup, and van Gaal left in April 2011. Bayern did not renew Altıntop's contract.

### What the Bayern years amounted to

Altıntop never became the "leading player" he had hoped to be. UEFA's summary when he left was that he had "not held down a regular starting role" in recent seasons. But four seasons at Bayern produced two Bundesliga titles, two DFB-Pokals, a Ligapokal, a Supercup and a Champions League final start, and three coaches with very different ideas all used him. His value lay in the versatility Hitzfeld had named on day one: right midfield, left midfield, central midfield or right-back, as the team needed.

## Euro 2008 and the Puskás Award

At Euro 2008 Turkey reached the semi-finals of a European Championship for the first time, and Altıntop was central to how they did it. He started the tournament at right-back and was moved into midfield.

Against the Czech Republic in Geneva on 15 June, Turkey were 2-0 down after 62 minutes. Altıntop's cross set up Arda Turan's goal, his cross was dropped by Petr Čech for Nihat Kahveci's equaliser three minutes from time, and his pass sent Nihat through for the winner two minutes later, according to [UEFA's match report](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/season=2008/matches/round=15093/match=300699/postmatch/report/index.html). Turkey won 3-2.

In the quarter-final against Croatia in Vienna on 20 June, Semih Şentürk equalised in stoppage time at the end of extra time, and Altıntop scored Turkey's third penalty in a 3-1 shootout win. UEFA named him man of the match. (How shootouts are run is covered in our guide to [extra time and penalty shootouts](/guides/extra-time-and-penalty-shootouts-explained).) Turkey lost the semi-final 3-2 to Germany in Basel on 25 June to a 90th-minute Philipp Lahm goal, and UEFA's technical team then [named Altıntop](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/news/0253-0d7c22bc5467-00c3bda04e75-1000--uefa-euro-2008tm-team-of-the-tournament) in its 23-man Team of the Tournament, alongside Luka Modrić, Xavi and Michael Ballack among the midfielders.

Two years later came the goal. On 3 September 2010, in Turkey's first Euro 2012 qualifier, in Astana, Emre Belözoğlu swung in a corner and Altıntop met it with a first-time volley that flew into the net in a [3-0 win over Kazakhstan](https://www.uefa.com/news/newsid=1516021.html). In January 2011 it was named goal of the year at the FIFA Ballon d'Or Gala after topping a poll of one million fans, ahead of nominees including Lionel Messi and his Bayern team-mate Arjen Robben. The trophy was handed to him by Andrei Sidelnikov, the Kazakhstan goalkeeper he had beaten.

His own verdict, given to [UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0254-0d7cf8cb9a50-2cc50c82c7b6-1000--hamit-altintop-joy-at-goal-of-the-year-award): it "might not have been the most important" goal of his career, but it was "certainly the most spectacular."

## Real Madrid, Galatasaray and Darmstadt

On 19 May 2011 Real Madrid confirmed that Altıntop would join on a free transfer when his Bayern contract expired on 30 June, on a [four-year deal](https://www.uefa.com/news/0254-0d7ce90c5c10-be1214eacb3f-1000--madrid-swoop-for-free-agent-hamit-altintop). It was the second free transfer of his career to a giant, and the rules that made both moves possible are explained in [free transfers and the Bosman ruling](/guides/free-transfers-and-the-bosman-ruling).

Under José Mourinho, behind Sami Khedira, Xabi Alonso and Mesut Özil, he played only 12 times. His five La Liga appearances brought one goal, in a 6-3 win at Sevilla, and Madrid won the 2011-12 title.

In July 2012 he moved to Galatasaray on a four-year contract for a [reported €3.5m](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/newsid=1841117.html), with the club saying he wanted to work under Fatih Terim. The highlight came against his old club. On 12 March 2013, in the Champions League last 16 in Gelsenkirchen, his 25-yard free-kick hit the post and went in to make it 1-1 on the night; Galatasaray won 3-2 and [went through 4-3 on aggregate](https://www.rte.ie/sport/soccer/2013/0312/376383-galatasaray-advance-at-schalkes-expense). Over his time in Istanbul he won two Süper Lig titles and two Turkish Cups, though injuries again limited his playing time.

It ended badly. On 28 December 2016 Galatasaray lost 3-2 in the Turkish Cup to third-tier Tuzlaspor; Altıntop played 61 minutes and was singled out by fans, some of whom claimed he had links to Tuzlaspor's management, which [the club called baseless](https://www.ntv.com.tr/spor/tuzlaspordan-hamit-altintop-aciklamasi,25MSWjDLFEm1X5YJpW0xnA). His contract was ended in early January 2017.

On 31 January 2017 he signed for Darmstadt, then bottom of the Bundesliga, on a [deal to the end of the season](https://www.abendzeitung-muenchen.de/sport/fussball/ex-bayer-altintop-wechselt-nach-darmstadt-art-381740,PRINT); coach Torsten Frings called the signing something special. Darmstadt were relegated, and after 34 matches for them he retired in 2018.

## Honours

- Bundesliga: 2007-08, 2009-10 (Bayern)
- DFB-Pokal: 2007-08, 2009-10 (Bayern)
- DFL-Ligapokal: 2005 (Schalke), 2007 (Bayern)
- DFL-Supercup: 2010 (Bayern)
- La Liga: 2011-12 (Real Madrid)
- Süper Lig: two titles (Galatasaray)
- Turkish Cup: two (Galatasaray)
- Champions League runner-up: 2009-10 (Bayern)
- UEFA Euro 2008 Team of the Tournament
- FIFA Puskás Award: 2010

## After playing: running Turkey's national teams

Altıntop moved into administration with the Turkish Football Federation, where he sat on the board. In September 2021, after Şenol Güneş's departure as national coach, the federation [gave him responsibility](https://www.istiklal.com.tr/spor/senol-gunesle-yollar-boyle-ayrildi-iste-altintop-ayrintisi-648109h) for the national teams, and Stefan Kuntz was appointed head coach within days. He was still the board member overseeing the national teams [at Euro 2024](https://www.aspor.com.tr/euro-2024/haberler/2024/06/15/hamit-altintop-ferdi-kadioglunun-sakatligi/amp). By July 2026 [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/turkiye/hamit-altintopdan-milli-takim-ve-montella-aciklamasi-burada-egolarin-yeri-yok-2629770) described him as a former federation official who had spent the previous year living in Los Angeles.
