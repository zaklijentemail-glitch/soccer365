**Illan Meslier is a French goalkeeper who played 215 games for Leeds United between 2019 and 2026, winning the Championship twice, and joined Arsenal on a free transfer in July 2026.** Born in Lorient on 2 March 2000, he set Premier League clean-sheet records for a young goalkeeper in 2020-21, shared the Championship Golden Glove in 2023-24 and kept 21 clean sheets in Leeds' 100-point title season of 2024-25.

Then it unravelled. Errors in spring 2025 cost him his place, he did not play a first-team game in 2025-26, and he left Elland Road when his contract ran out. His career so far is a story of two halves, and this profile covers both.

## Lorient: the hometown club

Meslier came through the academy at FC Lorient, the club of the Breton town where he was born, and [signed his first professional contract](https://www.arsenal.com/news/illan-meslier-signs-for-arsenal-aSP1T2x5SP7c) in February 2018. Later that year, aged 18, he made his senior debut and kept a clean sheet in a 1-0 Coupe de la Ligue win over Valenciennes.

He was Lorient's first choice in 2018-19, playing 28 times in Ligue 2 and [30 times in all competitions](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/illan-meslier-signs-on-loan), with 11 clean sheets. By then he had also been capped by France at under-18 and under-19 level. That one season in the French second tier was enough to take him to England.

## Leeds United, 2019-2026

### The loan and the first title

On 8 August 2019 Leeds signed Meslier from Lorient on a season-long loan. His debut came in January 2020 in an FA Cup third-round tie at the Emirates, the ground he would eventually call home.

His league debut was a 4-0 win over Hull City, in which he made four saves, and he went on to start 10 of Leeds' last 11 matches of 2019-20, [conceding only five goals](https://www.leedsunited.com/news/illan-meslier-to-leave-leeds-united). Seven of his 10 Championship games were clean sheets as Leeds won the title and returned to the Premier League. On 23 July 2020 the move [was made permanent](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/leeds-united-complete-illan-meslier-transfer) on a three-year contract.

### 2020-21: the records

Meslier's first top-flight season remains the high point of his Premier League career. He played 35 league games and kept 11 clean sheets as Leeds finished ninth, and he broke two age records along the way.

The first was set before he turned 21 on 2 March 2021: eight clean sheets in a season by a goalkeeper under 21, which broke a Premier League record [Joe Hart had held for 14 years](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/illan-meslier-pens-new-long-term-contract). The second came in April 2021, when a draw with Manchester United made him the [youngest goalkeeper to reach 10 Premier League clean sheets](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/illan-meslier-i-have-to-say-thanks-to-the-team). His reaction was to credit the side in front of him: "alone I can't do anything, but the team help me so, thank you Leeds!"

He was named Leeds' Young Player of the Year for 2020-21, and on 13 August 2021 he signed a new five-year contract that ran to the summer of 2026. That date matters: it is why he could leave for nothing five years later. How a player walks away at the end of a deal is explained in our guide to [free transfers and the Bosman ruling](/guides/free-transfers-and-the-bosman-ruling).

### Relegation and the Championship years

Leeds' Premier League stay ended after three seasons. On 28 May 2023 they lost 4-1 at home to Tottenham on the final day and [were relegated](https://www.skysports.com/football/leeds-united-vs-tottenham-hotspur/465011); Joel Robles was in goal that day.

Back in the Championship, Meslier was first choice again, and 2023-24 was his best individual season. He kept 18 league clean sheets and shared the Championship Golden Glove with West Brom's Alex Palmer, [the EFL confirmed](https://wba.co.uk/news/alex-palmer-presented-sky-bet-championship-golden-glove-award) on 10 May 2024. (Leeds' own count of his clean sheets that season is 19, in 43 league games.) He was named in the [PFA Championship Team of the Year](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/ethan-and-illan-named-in-pfas-championship-team-of-the-season). The season still ended in defeat: Leeds lost the play-off final 1-0 to Southampton at Wembley on 26 May 2024, Adam Armstrong scoring in the 24th minute ([Sky Sports report](https://www.skysports.com/football/leeds-united-vs-southampton/report/504570)).

### 2024-25: 21 clean sheets, then dropped

On paper, 2024-25 was a triumph. Meslier kept 21 clean sheets in 39 matches and played a key role in a record 100-point campaign as Leeds won the Championship and promotion, according to the club.

But it ended without him. In a 2-2 home draw with Swansea in March 2025 he dropped Josh Tymon's corner just after the hour for Harry Darling to score, and in the 96th minute Zan Vipotnik's shot slipped under his glove for the equaliser. The result stopped Leeds moving level on points with Sheffield United at the top. Farke [made the change](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11095/13341273/leeds-illan-meslier-dropped-for-saturdays-trip-to-luton-after-latest-high-profile-error) for the next game at Luton: "I have taken my decision and spoken to the goalkeepers, Karl Darlow will play Saturday." He insisted it was not a punishment: "we win together, lose together and draw together."

Sky Sports later counted three errors leading directly to goals in his Championship season, and Darlow kept the place as Leeds finished the job.

### 2025-26: the lost season

Back in the Premier League in 2025-26, Meslier was third choice behind Karl Darlow and Lucas Perri, and he made [no first-team appearances](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11670/13561360/illan-meslier-to-arsenal-gunners-set-to-sign-former-leeds-goalkeeper-on-free-transfer) all season. On 10 June 2026 Leeds confirmed he would leave when his contract expired, with a farewell line that "he will always be part of our proud history."

## Arsenal: a fresh start as No.30

On 9 July 2026 [Arsenal announced](https://www.arsenal.com/news/illan-meslier-signs-for-arsenal-aSP1T2x5SP7c) Meslier's arrival on a free transfer, subject to regulatory processes, and handed him the number 30 shirt. He called himself "extremely happy" to join "the biggest club in England" and said he wanted to "win trophies with this team".

The role on arrival was clear. Sky Sports reported that he was expected to be third choice behind David Raya, the first choice, and Kepa Arrizabalaga, a move that would let 20-year-old Tommy Setford go out on loan. Sky also understood Meslier would be open to becoming a No.1 elsewhere if the right club came in. Raya's own standing is covered in our ranking of the [best goalkeepers in the world in 2026](/guides/best-goalkeepers-in-the-world).

His debut, when it came, was a night to forget. On 27 October 2026 Arsenal, the Premier League champions, went to League Two Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup fourth round. Aaron Loupalo-Bi beat Meslier in the 21st minute, Viktor Gyökeres equalised in the 50th, and [Max Dowman](/guides/max-dowman-profile) was sent off late on. Fleetwood scored all five of their penalties and won the shootout 5-3, Jay Lynch saving from Bruno Guimarães ([Irish Examiner report](https://www.irishexaminer.com/sport/soccer/arid-41929866.html)).

## What his record says

His record splits cleanly by division. His two biggest clean-sheet seasons, 2023-24 and 2024-25, came in the Championship, where Leeds reached the play-off final and then won the title, while his age records came in his first top-flight season, 2020-21. His career also shows how quickly a keeper's standing can turn: from the Golden Glove to the bench in less than a year, and from Leeds' No.1 to a season without a game.
