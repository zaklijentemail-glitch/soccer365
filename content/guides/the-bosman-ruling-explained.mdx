The Bosman ruling was a 1995 European Court of Justice decision that gave footballers the right to move to a new club for free once their contract expired.

Before the ruling, clubs could demand a fee even for players out of contract. Since Bosman, free transfers at the end of a deal have become one of the biggest levers in the market.
