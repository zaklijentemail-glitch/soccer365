**The best left-back in the world in 2026 is PSG's Nuno Mendes**, who won the Champions League and Ligue 1 and was the left-back in UEFA's Champions League Team of the Season, with Inter's record-breaking Federico Dimarco and Manchester City's Nico O'Reilly next. Below is the full top ten, with a stat line for each player from the 2025-26 season.

## How the list is ranked

Three things, in this order:

1. **The 2025-26 club season.** League appearances as a measure of availability, the trophies the player's team won, and the end-of-season selections that judge individual players: UEFA's Champions League Team of the Season, the PFA Team of the Year, the UNFP Ligue 1 Team of the Year and the Serie A Top 11 voted by the players' union.
2. **The 2026 World Cup.** It is the most recent evidence there is, so it counts. Players whose countries did not qualify, Dimarco and Miloš Kerkez among them, are not marked down for that alone.
3. **Goals and assists**, where they help separate two close cases. A left-back is a defender first, so they never decide a place on their own.

Only players who mainly played on the left of the defence in 2025-26, as a full-back or a wing-back, are eligible. Reputation and transfer value are not criteria. Stat lines are for 2025-26 and give league appearances and goals, then all competitions.

## 1. Nuno Mendes (PSG, Portugal)

**2025-26:** 20 Ligue 1 appearances, 4 goals; 41 appearances, 6 goals in all competitions.

Twenty league games is low for a No.1, and injuries explain it. A [knee sprain against Bayern](https://beta.thescore.com/fra_fed/news/3388722/psgs-dembele-hakimi-mendes-expected-to-miss-several-weeks) on 4 November 2025 cost him several weeks, and thigh problems followed in the spring. He was also rested on the night PSG [clinched the title with a 2-0 win at Lens](https://ligue1.com/en/articles/l1_article_5109-recap-psg-are-champions-after-win-in-lens) on 13 May.

In the games that decided the season, though, he was there. He played 17 times in the Champions League, with two goals, and started the [final against Arsenal](https://www.skysports.com/football/paris-saint-germain-vs-arsenal/report/554950) in Budapest on 30 May. PSG won 4-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw, even though David Raya saved his kick. He was the left-back in [UEFA's Champions League Team of the Season](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/02a5-20c1d7d33d69-be4f6ab11b0a-1000--2025-26-uefa-champions-league-team-of-the-season/) and in the [UNFP Ligue 1 Team of the Year](https://news.maxifoot.fr/trophees-unfp/une-equipe-type-parisio-lensoise-foot-455776.htm), and was on the shortlist for the UNFP's Player of the Year, which went to Ousmane Dembélé. He also scored in the shootouts that won PSG the UEFA Super Cup against Tottenham and the Trophée des Champions against Marseille.

At the World Cup he started all five of Portugal's games and scored a [direct free kick in the 5-0 win over Uzbekistan](https://www.psg.fr/en/content/with-nuno-mendes-on-the-scoresheet-portugal-thrash-uzbekistan-paris-saint-germain-fifa-world-cup-20252026). In the round of 16 against Spain he went off after 56 minutes with what [media reports described as a thigh problem](https://www.theweek.in/news/health/2026/07/08/fifa-world-cup-2026-or-what-happened-to-portugals-nuno-mendes-during-the-clash-against-spain.html), and Portugal lost 1-0.

## 2. Federico Dimarco (Inter, Italy)

**2025-26:** 35 Serie A appearances, 6 goals, 18 assists; 47 appearances in all competitions.

Dimarco had the most productive season of anyone on this list. His 18 league assists [set a Serie A record](https://en.legaseriea.it/serie-a/news/dimarco-professione-assistman) for the three-points-for-a-win era, beating the 16 shared by Papu Gómez (2019-20) and Ronaldinho (2009-10). With his six goals that made 24 goal contributions, the [highest total in the league](https://www.inter.it/en/news/federico-dimarco-serie-a-mvp-2025-26), and he was named Serie A's MVP, the first defender to win the award. He was also the left-back in the [Serie A Top 11](https://sport.sky.it/calcio/serie-a/2026/09/02/gran-gala-calcio-2026-top-11-serie-a) voted by the players.

Inter won the league and the Coppa Italia, beating Lazio 2-0 in the final on 13 May, and Dimarco played the full 90 minutes. Lega Serie A describes him as a left wing-back in Inter's [3-5-2](/guides/3-5-2-formation-explained), and that is the one caveat: his job is closer to a winger's than to a back-four full-back's.

What keeps him off the top spot is Europe and the summer. Inter were not a Champions League finalist, and Italy did not reach the World Cup, losing the play-off final [on penalties in Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://www.uefa.com/european-qualifiers/news/02a3-2047c06e29c6-0a8f1c84272a-1000--bosnia-and-herzegovina-1-1-italy-4-3-on-pens-hosts-win-2026) on 31 March. Dimarco started that game.

## 3. Nico O'Reilly (Manchester City, England)

**2025-26:** 34 Premier League appearances, 5 goals, 3 assists; 53 appearances, 9 goals in all competitions.

O'Reilly is the left-back in the [PFA Team of the Year](https://www.arsenal.com/news/five-gunners-named-in-pfa-team-of-the-year-axXNQ7K6Jpdf), the only defender in it not from Arsenal. He also won the PFA Young Player of the Year and the [Premier League's Young Player of the Season](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4662697/oreilly-named-young-player-of-the-season), at 21.

He decided the [League Cup final](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13523071/arsenal-0-2-man-city-player-ratings-nico-oreilly-faultless-as-mikel-artetas-side-choke-in-league-cup-final-at-wembley) on 22 March, scoring both goals in City's 2-0 win over the eventual champions, and started the 1-0 FA Cup final win over Chelsea on 16 May. Pep Guardiola has used him in more than one position: "He's played full-back, played holding midfielder when Rodri was not there."

At the World Cup he played seven times and started five for England, who finished third. He lost his place to Djed Spence for the 2-1 semi-final defeat by Argentina and came on in the 83rd minute.

## 4. Marc Cucurella (Chelsea, Spain)

**2025-26:** 34 Premier League appearances, 1 goal; 50 appearances, 1 goal in all competitions.

On club form alone Cucurella would be lower. Chelsea won nothing, losing the FA Cup final to City, and he missed about three weeks with a [hamstring injury](https://worldsoccertalk.com/amp/news/chelsea-boss-rosenior-issues-worrying-injury-update-on-marc-cucurella-ahead-of-spains-finalissima) against Leeds in February.

The World Cup is what lifts him. He started all eight of Spain's matches at left-back, including the final against Argentina on 19 July, which [Spain won 1-0](https://prod.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/fifa-world-cup-2026/articles/confirmed-lineups-for-spain-and-argentina-in-the-fifa-world-cup-final-2026-07-19) after extra time, and he played all 120 minutes of it. He was the left-back in the World Cup best XI chosen by [FIFA's fan vote](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/articles/fifa-world-cup-2026-best-xi-which-players-made-the-official-dream-team-2026-07-22). Real Madrid had already signed him: he [joined from Chelsea](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13554263/marc-cucurella-transfer-news-real-madrid-sign-defender-from-chelsea-in-deal-worth-lb51-8m) on 15 June in a deal Sky Sports reported as worth up to £51.8m.

## 5. Alejandro Grimaldo (Bayer Leverkusen, Spain)

**2025-26:** 29 Bundesliga appearances, 8 goals; 46 appearances, 14 goals in all competitions.

Nobody on this list scored more. Grimaldo finished with [14 goals and 12 assists](https://bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/world-cup-stars-alejandro-grimaldo-spain-bayer-leverkusen-36727) in all competitions, numbers that would be good for a winger. Leverkusen gave him little to show for it, finishing sixth, and at the World Cup he was Cucurella's understudy and did not play. He [left for Atlético Madrid](https://www.bayer04.de/en-us/news/bayer04/farewell-to-a-star-player-grimaldo-leaves-bayer-04-for-atletico-madrid) at the end of June.

## 6. Piero Hincapié (Arsenal, Ecuador)

**2025-26:** 25 Premier League appearances, 1 goal; 39 appearances, 1 goal in all competitions for Arsenal.

Arsenal [won the Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4662306/arsenal-win-2025-26-premier-league-title-ending-22-year-wait-to-be-crowned-champions-again) for the first time in 22 years, sharing left-back between Hincapié and Riccardo Calafiori, who made 26 league appearances. Hincapié gets the place because he was the one Mikel Arteta picked for the big games: he started both the League Cup final and the Champions League final at left-back. Arsenal lost both. Sky Sports' verdict on the first was that he "looked every inch a centre-back playing out of position". His loan from Leverkusen was [made permanent](https://www.arsenal.com/news/arsenal-transfers-all-the-ins-and-outs-in-202627-a8qx29v8B1fR) from 1 July. Ecuador went out of the World Cup in the round of 32, losing 2-0 to Mexico, and he was sent off in stoppage time.

## 7. Alejandro Balde (Barcelona, Spain)

**2025-26:** 28 La Liga appearances, 0 goals; 42 appearances, 0 goals in all competitions.

Barcelona [won La Liga](https://www.fcbarcelona.com/en/football/first-team/news/4501708/the-29th-league-title-in-fc-barcelona-history) with 94 points and beat Real Madrid 3-2 in the Supercopa final in January, where Balde [started at left-back](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Deportes/Barcelona-se-corono-campeon-de-la-Supercopa-de-Espana-al-vencer-al-Real-Madrid-3-2-uc949312). A [hamstring strain](https://www.football-espana.net/2025/09/03/barcelona-confirm-alejandro-balde-hamstring-injury) in September was the only interruption. Against that, he was not in La Liga's team of the season and was left out of Spain's World Cup squad.

## 8. Nicolás Tagliafico (Lyon, Argentina)

**2025-26:** 20 Ligue 1 appearances, 0 goals, 3 assists; 30 appearances, 0 goals in all competitions.

Tagliafico's club season was modest. His summer was not. A [calf strain](https://www.minutouno.com/deportes/seleccion-argentina-nicolas-tagliafico-sufrio-una-lesion-muscular-y-peligra-su-participacion-n6287906) on 11 June kept him out of Argentina's opening group games, but he started the last-16 and quarter-final wins over Egypt and Switzerland, the semi-final win over England and [the final](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/sports/fifa-world-cup/fifa-world-cup-argentina-squad-lineup-changes-spain-final?amp=1). Sports Mole noted he marked Lamine Yamal for all 120 minutes of the 1-0 defeat.

## 9. Theo Hernández (Al-Hilal, France)

**2025-26:** 31 Saudi Pro League appearances, 5 goals; 44 appearances, 9 goals in all competitions.

Theo scored in the King's Cup final, a 2-1 win over Al-Kholood on 8 May, and Al-Hilal finished two points behind [champions Al-Nassr](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/sports/football/ronaldo-al-nassr-win-saudi-league-title-4-1-damac). He started France's first two World Cup games, then [lost his place to Lucas Digne](https://www.maxifoot.fr/football/article-65196.htm) after errors, and was back for the third-place defeat by England.

## 10. Miloš Kerkez (Liverpool, Hungary)

**2025-26:** 34 Premier League appearances, 2 goals; 48 appearances, 2 goals in all competitions.

Kerkez makes the list on availability: among the ten, only Dimarco played more league games than his 34. Liverpool finished fifth and won nothing, and Hungary missed the World Cup after a [3-2 defeat by Ireland](https://dailynewshungary.com/hungary-misses-2026-world-cup-defeat/) in November, in which his cross set up a goal.

## Just missing out

**Riccardo Calafiori** (Arsenal) was a Premier League champion with 26 league appearances, but [Arsenal said in January](https://www.arsenal.com/news/artetas-update-calafiori-hincapie-and-havertz) he and Hincapié had both been out since the start of 2026, and Hincapié started the finals.

**Alphonso Davies** (Bayern Munich) returned from a torn ACL in December and then had [three muscle injuries](https://ianslive.in/alphonso-davies-ruled-out-for-weeks-with-hamstring-injury-confirms-bayern-munich--20260508181720), the last in the Champions League semi-final against PSG. He made 23 appearances as Bayern won the double, and at the World Cup played the last 15 minutes of Canada's round-of-32 win over South Africa.

**Álvaro Carreras** (Real Madrid) played 28 La Liga games in his first season at the club, but Madrid won nothing and he was not in Spain's World Cup squad.

## What the list says about the position

The top three do different jobs. Mendes plays in a back four; Dimarco is a wing-back who supplies chances like a winger; O'Reilly has played full-back and holding midfield under Guardiola. Our guide to [full-backs, wing-backs and inverted full-backs](/guides/fullback-vs-wingback-vs-inverted-fullback) explains how those jobs differ.

For the other flank, see [the best right-backs in the world in 2026](/guides/best-right-backs-in-the-world), where Mendes's PSG teammate Achraf Hakimi is No.1.
