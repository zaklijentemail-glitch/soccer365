**The best striker in the world in 2026 is Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappé**, who won the World Cup Golden Boot with 10 goals and broke the tournament's all-time scoring record, with Barcelona's World Cup-winning substitute Ferran Torres and Inter's Serie A top scorer Lautaro Martínez close behind. Below is the full top ten, with a stat line for each player from the 2025-26 season.

## How the list is ranked

Three things, in this order:

1. **The 2025-26 club season.** Goals, the trophies the player's team won, and the top-scorer prizes that judge individual forwards: the Pichichi in Spain, the Capocannoniere in Italy, and the Bundesliga and Premier League scoring charts.
2. **The 2026 World Cup.** It is the most recent evidence there is, so it counts. Players whose countries did not qualify are not marked down for that alone.
3. **Goals and assists**, where they help separate two close cases.

Reputation and transfer value are not criteria. Stat lines are for 2025-26 and note whether the figure is league-only or across all competitions, since not every club publishes the same breakdown.

## 1. Kylian Mbappé (Real Madrid, France)

**2025-26:** 31 La Liga appearances, 25 goals, 5 assists for Real Madrid; 10 goals in 7 matches to win the World Cup Golden Boot.

Mbappé finished as [La Liga's top scorer](https://www.laliga.com/en-US/player/kylian-mbappe-lottin) for a second straight season, even though his tally dropped from 31 goals in 2024-25, and Real Madrid finished second behind a dominant Barcelona side on 94 points. At the World Cup, he carried France to the semi-final before [a scoreless display in a 2-0 loss to Spain](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/sports/fifa-world-cup/fifa-world-cup-semifinal-loss-france-vs-spain-kylian-mbappe-reaction), then scored twice in a wild 6-4 third-place play-off defeat to England to [seal the Golden Boot](https://skysports.com/football/news/11095/13564837/world-cup-2026-golden-boot-race-kylian-mbappe-beats-lionel-messi-to-become-top-goalscorer) with 10 tournament goals. Those two goals also took him to 22 career World Cup goals, [the all-time record](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/49397919/kylian-mbappe-wins-golden-boot-2026-world-cup-lionel-messi-goalless-final), and made him the first player to win the Golden Boot at two different World Cups. No team trophy this season, but no individual case is stronger.

## 2. Ferran Torres (Barcelona, Spain)

**2025-26:** 21 goals, 3 assists in 51 appearances across all competitions for Barcelona.

An injury to Robert Lewandowski in the early weeks of the season [opened the door](https://www.si.com/soccer/do-barcelona-really-need-a-striker) for Torres to become Barcelona's starting centre-forward, and he kept the shirt as the club won La Liga with 94 points, 8 clear of Real Madrid. His World Cup was quieter individually, just 1 goal in 8 appearances for Spain, but it was the goal that mattered: coming off the bench in the final against Argentina, he scored the winner in the [106th minute of extra time](https://www.sofascore.com/news/ferran-torres-seals-spains-world-cup-final-win) as Spain won the tournament 1-0. A league title and a World Cup winner's medal, scored by his own boot, is a season almost nobody else on this list can match.

## 3. Lautaro Martínez (Inter Milan, Argentina)

**2025-26:** 17 goals in 30 Serie A appearances, the league's top scorer.

Inter's [Capocannoniere](https://www.inter.it/en/news/lautaro-martinez-top-scorer-serie-a-2025-26) fired Cristian Chivu's side to a [21st Scudetto](https://www.flashscore.com/news/soccer-serie-a-chivu-s-imperious-inter-milan-clinch-serie-a-title-with-three-rounds-to-spare/l0PNSmb4/), confirmed with three matches still to play. At the World Cup he came off the bench to score Argentina's stoppage-time winner against England in the semi-final, turning the game around after [Lionel Messi's two assists](https://www.thescore.com/worldcup/news/3566881/sms:) in seven second-half minutes, and finished the tournament with three goals and an assist as Argentina reached the final before losing 1-0 to Spain. A league and scoring title plus a World Cup final appearance is a season built on substance, not just one big moment.

## 4. Harry Kane (Bayern Munich, England)

**2025-26:** 36 goals in 31 Bundesliga appearances, plus 10 goals in 6 DFB-Pokal matches.

Kane was [crowned Bundesliga top scorer for a third year running](https://fcbayern.com/en/news/2026/05/harry-kane-crowned-bundesliga-top-scorer-for-third-year-running) as Bayern finished the season well clear of Borussia Dortmund, who were runners-up on 73 points. England's captain then led his country to the World Cup semi-final, where [Lionel Messi's late show](https://www.sportinglife.com/football/news/england-1-2-argentina-lionel-messi-late-show-breaks-three-lions-hearts/233244) saw Argentina come from behind to win 2-1. Kane was among the tournament's leading scorers throughout, and England regrouped to beat France 6-4 in the third-place play-off, taking World Cup bronze. A league title, a personal scoring crown and a World Cup medal, even if it isn't gold, is a season most strikers would take.

## 5. Erling Haaland (Manchester City, Norway)

**2025-26:** 27 Premier League goals in 35 appearances, plus 8 goals in 10 Champions League matches.

Haaland won the Premier League's Golden Boot again, and [Manchester City won a domestic cup double](https://www.si.com/soccer/man-city-win-2025-26-fa-cup-victory-chelsea), beating [Arsenal in the League Cup final](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/man-city-outclass-arsenal-guardiola-wins-record-5th-league-cup/amp) and Chelsea in the FA Cup final, even as Arsenal pipped City to the league title itself, 85 points to 78. His World Cup was the story of the tournament's first half: he scored twice on his debut in a [4-1 win over Iraq](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/12098/13552715/world-cup-2026-iraq-1-4-norway-erling-haaland-scores-twice-on-tournament-debut-as-stale-solbakkens-side-make-winning-start), added a brace in the last 16 to [stun five-time champions Brazil 2-1](https://www.aljazeera.com/sports/2026/7/5/haaland-scores-twice-as-norway-stun-brazil-2-1-in-world-cup-2026-last-16), and took Norway to their first-ever World Cup quarter-final, a tournament their country had not even reached since 1998. Norway's run ended there, beaten 2-1 after extra time by England, with Haaland finishing on 7 goals. A trophy, a Golden Boot and a piece of his country's football history.

## 6. Victor Osimhen (Galatasaray, Nigeria)

**2025-26:** 15 Süper Lig goals in 22 appearances; 22 goals and 8 assists across all competitions.

Osimhen scored both goals, including a stoppage-time winner, as Galatasaray [beat Antalyaspor 4-2](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/galatasaray-cement-super-lig-domination-with-record-26th-title/amp) to clinch a 26th Süper Lig title, the club's fourth in a row. He was not the league's outright top scorer, finishing behind Paul Onuachu, Eldor Shomurodov and Anderson Talisca, but Galatasaray won 20 of the 24 league games in which he scored. Nigeria's World Cup dream ended in November 2025, losing the CAF play-off final to DR Congo on penalties, so there is no tournament to weigh here. A fourth straight title with his club is still a season most strikers in Europe would envy.

## 7. Serhou Guirassy (Borussia Dortmund, Guinea)

**2025-26:** 17 Bundesliga goals in 33 appearances, third behind Harry Kane and Deniz Undav in the league's scoring charts.

Guirassy's goals [fired Dortmund up the table](https://prod.beinsports.com/en-nz/football/bundesliga/articles-video/guirassy-ends-drought-as-dortmund-goes-second-2025-10-31) through the season, and Dortmund finished as Bundesliga runners-up, 73 points from their 34 matches, enough to secure Champions League qualification but short of Bayern Munich. There is no World Cup to add to his case and no major individual prize, since 17 goals was not enough to top the Bundesliga's own scoring list this season. On pure numbers for a side that competed at the top of one of Europe's best leagues, he still edges several more decorated names below him.

## 8. Viktor Gyökeres (Arsenal, Sweden)

**2025-26:** 14 Premier League goals in 35 appearances for the champions.

Gyökeres was part of an [Arsenal side that won the Premier League](https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48817408/) for the first time in 22 years, with 85 points from their 38 games. Before that, in March, his 88th-minute winner in a [3-2 play-off defeat of Poland](https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/news/sweden-vs-poland-recap-sweden-qualify-for-2026-world-cup-gyokeres-elanga) sent Sweden to their first World Cup since 2018. He scored and assisted in [Sweden's 5-1 win over Tunisia](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/isak-gyokeres-dominate-as-sweden-overwhelm-tunisia-in-5-1-win/amp) in the group stage, but Sweden's run ended in the round of 32, [beaten 3-0 by France](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/sports/fifa-wc-2026-france-beat-sweden-30-end-their-world-cup-campaign-articleshow-a84hf8d). A league title with Arsenal and the goal that ended an eight-year wait for a World Cup place are the highlights of a season where his club goal tally was modest by this list's standards.

## 9. Robert Lewandowski (Barcelona, Poland)

**2025-26:** 18 goals, 2 assists in 44 appearances across all competitions for the La Liga champions.

At 37, Lewandowski lost his starting place to Ferran Torres early in the season after injury, and what [Sofascore called a quieter campaign](https://www.sofascore.com/news/the-end-of-lewandowskis-barcelona-era-four-years-of-goals-and-big-moments) than his previous three years at the club still ended with a league winner's medal as Barcelona ran away with the title. His country's World Cup hopes ended before the tournament even began: Poland lost the UEFA play-off final 3-2 to Sweden, with [Gyökeres scoring the winner](https://www.thescore.com/news/3512065/sms:) that eliminated them. A title medal from a rotation role, with no World Cup to show for it, is still enough to make this list ahead of several out-and-out starters elsewhere.

## 10. Alexander Isak (Liverpool, Sweden)

**2025-26:** Around 4 goals in 22 appearances across all competitions in an injury-hit first season at Liverpool.

Isak's move to Liverpool became what one profile called [a major disappointment](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/2438), with fitness problems limiting him to a fraction of the appearances expected of a club-record signing, and no domestic honours to show for it. He was still named in Sweden's World Cup squad alongside Gyökeres, and he got off the mark in the [5-1 win over Tunisia](https://www.dailysabah.com/sports/football/isak-gyokeres-dominate-as-sweden-overwhelm-tunisia-in-5-1-win/amp) in Monterrey before Sweden's round-of-32 exit to France. It is a wasted club season by his own standard, but his name and his World Cup minutes keep him on this list, for now.
