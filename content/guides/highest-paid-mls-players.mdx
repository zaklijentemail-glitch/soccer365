**Lionel Messi is the highest-paid player in Major League Soccer in 2026**, on $28,333,333 in guaranteed compensation at Inter Miami, more than double what anyone else in the league earns. The figures below come from the MLS Players Association's own salary guide, the one public record of what clubs pay their players, rather than estimates built from transfer gossip.

The ranking here follows the guide's own measure: guaranteed compensation, which is base salary plus signing bonuses and any other guaranteed payments, annualised across a contract and the number the league uses for salary-budget purposes. The figures are from the [spring 2026 salary guide](https://mlsplayers.org/resources/salary-guide), current as of 16 April 2026 and released on 12 May 2026; no fall 2026 edition had been published by the time of writing. Performance detail below is from the 2025 MLS season, the most recently completed one.

## 1. Lionel Messi (Inter Miami, Argentina)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $28,333,333. **2025 season:** 29 goals and 19 assists in 28 games.

Messi's MLS deal is in a category of its own: more than two and a half times Son Heung-min's guaranteed pay in second place. He earned it again in 2025, winning the [MLS Golden Boot](https://www.si.com/soccer/lionel-messi-wins-mls-golden-boot) with 29 goals, Inter Miami's first Golden Boot winner, and becoming the first player to win back-to-back MLS MVP awards after taking 70.43% of the vote over San Diego FC's Anders Dreyer. On 23 October 2025 he signed a contract extension with Inter Miami through the end of the 2028 season, so this salary has years left to run.

## 2. Son Heung-min (LAFC, South Korea)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $11,152,852. **2025 season:** three goals in the MLS Cup Playoffs.

Son's move from Tottenham Hotspur in 2025 was reported as an MLS transfer record for LAFC, and his guaranteed pay reflects it: still less than half of Messi's, but clear of every other MLS contract bar Inter Miami's two South Americans. He made an immediate impact after signing, and scored three times for LAFC once the 2025 playoffs arrived.

## 3. Rodrigo De Paul (Inter Miami, Argentina)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $9,688,320. **Career honour:** started Argentina's 2022 World Cup final win over France.

De Paul joined Inter Miami on loan from Atlético Madrid in the summer of 2025, reuniting him with Messi after the pair started together in the 2022 World Cup final, which Argentina won on penalties after a 3-3 draw with France. That World Cup-winning pedigree, plus the box-to-box running Inter Miami lack elsewhere in midfield, explains a guaranteed-pay figure that sits above every MLS player except his own captain and LAFC's marquee winger.

## 4. Hirving Lozano (San Diego FC, Mexico)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $9,333,333. **Signed:** San Diego FC's first Designated Player, from PSV Eindhoven, for a reported fee of around $12 million.

San Diego FC built its first-ever MLS roster around Lozano, announcing the signing in June 2024 before he officially joined on 1 January 2025 on a deal through 2028 with two option years. He had scored nine goals for the expansion club by the end of August 2025, and his guaranteed pay, the fourth-highest in the league, is the clearest sign of how much an expansion team will spend to make its first season matter.

## 5. Miguel Almirón (Atlanta United, Paraguay)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $7,871,000. **2025 season:** six goals, 31 league appearances (started every one).

Almirón returned to Atlanta United from Newcastle United in January 2025, in a deal reported at around $10 million, on a contract through 2027 with a 2028 club option. He started all 31 of Atlanta's league games in 2025, scoring six goals, and his pay reflects both his status as the club's marquee Designated Player and the premium MLS sides now pay to bring back a player who left for the Premier League and is willing to return.

## 6. Emil Forsberg (New York Red Bulls, Sweden)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $6,035,625. **2025 season:** named MLS Player of the Matchday for Matchday 12.

Forsberg has been one of the Red Bulls' most consistent attacking players, and the Matchday 12 award in 2025 was one of several recognitions during a season in which New York were still shaking off their run to the 2024 MLS Cup final, a 2-1 defeat to LA Galaxy. His guaranteed pay sits just above the league's next tier of Designated Players, reflecting a long-standing role as the creative core of a side still built to compete for MLS Cup itself.

## 7. Sam Surridge (Nashville SC, England)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $5,933,000. **2025 season:** 24 goals, tied for second in the Golden Boot race.

Surridge's 2025 season put him level with LAFC's Denis Bouanga for second place in MLS's scoring charts, behind only Messi, and made him one of four finalists for league MVP alongside Messi, Bouanga and San Diego's Anders Dreyer. For an out-and-out striker rather than a marquee creative Designated Player, that goal return is the most direct justification on this list for the size of the contract.

## 8. Riqui Puig (LA Galaxy, Spain)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $5,792,188. **Honour:** part of LA Galaxy's record sixth MLS Cup title in 2024.

Puig was central to the Galaxy's run to a league-record sixth MLS Cup in December 2024, a 2-1 win over the New York Red Bulls, even though a torn ACL suffered in the Western Conference final meant he had to watch the final itself from the sidelines. The Galaxy's willingness to keep him among the highest-paid players in the league despite that injury reflects how much his passing in midfield was missed when he was absent.

## 9. Jonathan Bamba (Chicago Fire, Côte d'Ivoire)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $5,581,806. **International career:** switched from France's youth set-up to make his senior Côte d'Ivoire debut on 14 October 2023, against Morocco.

Bamba is the name on this list furthest from matching his salary with end-product: through Matchday 9 of the 2025 season he had five assists, tied for only third in the league at that point in the year, rather than a season-defining goal or assist tally. His guaranteed pay is a reminder that MLS Designated Player money is as much about reputation and wage-budget mechanics, built from signing fees and bonuses amortised over a contract, as it is about a single season's output.

## 10. Hany Mukhtar (Nashville SC, Germany)

**Guaranteed compensation:** $5,411,667. **Honour:** 2022 Landon Donovan MLS Most Valuable Player, with the Golden Boot the same year.

Mukhtar's $5.41 million in guaranteed pay is still built largely on the season that made his name: 23 goals in 2022, enough to win both the Golden Boot and the league MVP award with 48.1% of the vote, well clear of Austin FC's Sebastián Driussi in second. He is Nashville's second entrant in the top 10 alongside Surridge, making it, with Inter Miami, one of only two clubs with two players in the league's top earners.

## What the next guide might change

The MLS Players Association has released a salary guide in both spring and autumn in recent years, so a fall 2026 edition, covering any mid-season signings and extensions, was still due at the time of writing. Historically that update has moved the table only at the margins rather than displacing Messi from the top spot: his guaranteed pay is large enough, and his extension through 2028 secure enough, that only a genuinely record-breaking signing elsewhere in the league would change who tops this list.
