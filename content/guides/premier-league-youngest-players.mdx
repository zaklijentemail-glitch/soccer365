**On the records available on 23 October 2026, the youngest player to have appeared in the 2026-27 Premier League is Chelsea's Mahdi Nicoll-Jazuli, born on 6 January 2010, who made his debut at Brentford on 18 September aged 16 years and 255 days.** Arsenal's Max Dowman, six days older and already the youngest scorer in the competition's history, has also played this season, and Manchester United's JJ Gabriel, who turned 16 on 6 October, is the youngest of the players tipped to debut next.

This page lists the youngest players in the 2026-27 picture by date of birth, sets out what each has done at senior level, and puts them against the all-time list of the Premier League's youngest debutants.

Four of the eight were born in 2010 or on the last day of 2009, and three of those four play for two London clubs, Chelsea and Arsenal.

## Mahdi Nicoll-Jazuli, Chelsea

Nicoll-Jazuli's debut came as a late substitute in a [3-0 defeat at Brentford](https://www.fotmob.com/matches/chelsea-vs-brentford/2spihp) on Friday 18 September 2026. At 16 years and 255 days he became Chelsea's youngest Premier League player, a record previously held by Jody Morris, and the first player born in 2010 to appear in the competition. He sits 16th on [The Analyst's all-time list](https://theanalyst.com/articles/youngest-premier-league-players) of the youngest Premier League debutants.

He is a central midfielder who has played as a No.6, a No.8 and a No.10. He was born in Sydney and has represented England at youth level, but [Football360](https://football360.com.au/mahdi-nicoll-jazuli-chelsea-brentford/) reports that he also holds a French passport, so his international future is not settled. At Chelsea he was already the youngest player to start for the club in the UEFA Youth League.

## Max Dowman, Arsenal

Dowman is six days older than Nicoll-Jazuli and already holds two of the competition's biggest age records. He scored against Everton on 14 March 2026 at 16 years and 73 days, which the [Premier League confirmed](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4613476/dowman-becomes-premier-leagues-youngest-ever-goalscorer) beat James Vaughan's record by 197 days. On the final day of 2025-26 he started against Crystal Palace and became the [youngest player ever to start a Premier League match](https://theanalyst.com/articles/youngest-premier-league-players), at 16 years and 144 days.

In 2026-27 he had made one Premier League appearance by mid-October, a nine-minute appearance according to [The Analyst's player data](https://theanalyst.com/football/player/sc-616077/max-dowman), plus two goals in a League Cup tie. His full record, contract situation and England youth career are covered in the [Max Dowman profile](/guides/max-dowman-profile).

## Reggie Watson, Chelsea

Watson, born on 26 January 2010, is younger than Nicoll-Jazuli but had no Premier League appearance on the records available at the time of writing. His senior debut was a start in Chelsea's League Cup third-round tie against Leeds United in September. He is a defensive midfielder and the son of the former midfielder Ben Watson, and the [Premier League picked him](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4686609/each-premier-league-clubs-potential-breakout-star-in-202627) as Chelsea's potential breakout player of the season after he was given first-team minutes in pre-season.

## JJ Gabriel, Manchester United

Gabriel is the youngest player on this list and the only one without a competitive first-team appearance. Born on 6 October 2010, he came on in the 82nd minute of United's pre-season friendly with Atlético Madrid in Stockholm on 1 August 2026, aged 15. He was named the Under-18 Premier League Player of the Season for 2025-26 after scoring more than 20 goals, and the [Premier League noted](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4686609/each-premier-league-clubs-potential-breakout-star-in-202627) that he would become Manchester United's youngest Premier League player if he made his debut this season.

His 16th birthday matters for a practical reason: it is the age at which he could [sign a scholarship agreement](https://www.unitedinfocus.com/news/when-jj-gabriel-can-officially-sign-a-scholarship-and-professional-deal-at-man-utd/) with the club. A professional contract has to wait until he turns 17.

## The 17- and 18-year-olds

**Brian Madjo (Aston Villa, born 12 January 2009).** Villa signed the forward from Metz in January 2026 for a reported £10m but had to go to the Court of Arbitration for Sport before they could register him. His competitive debut was the [2026 UEFA Super Cup](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/aston-villa/feature/who-is-madjo-the-wonderkid-who-terrorised-psgs-defence_602875.html) against Paris Saint-Germain on 12 August, when he scored just before half-time in a 2-1 defeat. At 17 years and 212 days he became the youngest scorer in the competition's history, overtaking Patrick Kluivert's 1996 record.

**Joél Drakes-Thomas (Crystal Palace, born 9 June 2009).** A right winger who became Crystal Palace's youngest Premier League player when he came on against Leeds United in December 2025, at 16 years and 194 days. That ranks him ninth on the all-time list. Two days earlier he had become the youngest English player to appear in the Conference League.

**Rio Ngumoha (Liverpool, born 29 August 2008).** The most established of the group. He scored a winner on his Premier League debut against Newcastle United in August 2025, made 29 first-team appearances last season and has since made his senior England debut.

**Lucá Williams-Barnett (Tottenham, born 1 October 2008).** An attacking midfielder who debuted in a League Cup tie against Doncaster Rovers and was the Premier League's pick as Tottenham's breakout player for 2026-27.

## Why clubs can play 16-year-olds so freely

Two rules shape how a 16-year-old fits into a Premier League squad.

**They cannot be professionals yet.** Players in English football cannot sign a professional contract until they turn 17. Before that they are on scholarship terms, which is why 16-year-olds such as Dowman, Nicoll-Jazuli and Watson play first-team football without professional contracts.

**They do not use up a squad place.** Each Premier League club names a 25-man squad list after each transfer window, with no more than 17 players who are not homegrown. But, as the [Premier League's own explainer](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4097471/premier-league-squad-lists-explained) puts it, "a club's Under-21 players are not included on the 25-man squad lists." A 16-year-old can be brought on in a league match without the club leaving out a senior player to make room on the list. How those lists and the homegrown quota work is explained in [Premier League squad rules](/guides/premier-league-squad-rules-explained).

The same rules explain why the academy players who do break through are so valuable, and why some are sold before they reach the first team; see [why clubs sell academy players](/guides/why-clubs-sell-academy-players). Jeremy Monga, third on the all-time list after his Leicester City debut, is covered in the [Jeremy Monga profile](/guides/jeremy-monga-profile).

## Who could be next

Beyond Gabriel and Watson, the [Premier League's preview of the season](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4686609/each-premier-league-clubs-potential-breakout-star-in-202627) named several 17-year-olds who had not yet played in the competition: Nottingham Forest winger Kalum Thompson, the club's scholar of the year, and Coventry City midfielder George Shepherd, who had been named on the bench for games against Blackburn and Watford. Arsenal's academy has already produced the two youngest debutants in the competition's history.
