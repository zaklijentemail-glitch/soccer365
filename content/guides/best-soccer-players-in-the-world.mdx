**The best soccer player in the world right now is Harry Kane**, who beat Kylian Mbappé and Lamine Yamal to the 2026 Ballon d'Or on 26 October at the London Palladium after scoring 61 goals in all competitions for Bayern Munich in 2025-26. Below is the full top ten, built around that podium and the season and World Cup that put each player on it.

## How the list is ranked

The Ballon d'Or is the one vote that ranks every player on the planet against each other once a year, so this list follows its 2026 podium in order: Kane, Mbappé, Yamal, Rodri, Messi, Kvaratskhelia, Dembélé, Haaland, Olise and Ruiz. Each entry below gives the result or stat line that explains the placing, drawn from the 2025-26 season just finished and the 2026 World Cup that followed it.

## 1. Harry Kane (Bayern Munich, England)

Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or at the London Palladium on 26 October, the first Englishman to take it since Michael Owen in 2001, finishing ahead of Mbappé and Yamal. The case is the 2025-26 season: 61 goals in all competitions, 19 more than any other player in Europe's top five leagues and the most by anyone since Cristiano Ronaldo's 61 for Real Madrid in 2014-15, according to [SoccerNews's report on the ceremony](https://www.soccernews.com/kane-scoops-first-ballon-dor-after-pipping-yamal-mbappe/407033/). He also took home the Gerd Müller Trophy, the Ballon d'Or's own prize for the season's top scorer, and took 73 goals for club and country combined across the year.

## 2. Kylian Mbappé (Real Madrid, France)

Mbappé finished second, as he did the season before, on the back of another individual scoring title: La Liga's Pichichi trophy, with 25 goals and 5 assists in 31 appearances in 2025-26. A single club prize kept him below Kane, but the margin at the top was close enough that Real Madrid's France forward remains the most decorated individual scorer in Europe outside the winner himself.

## 3. Lamine Yamal (Barcelona, Spain)

Yamal was third for the second year running, and picked up the Kopa Trophy, awarded alongside the main prize to the best player aged 21 or under, for a third consecutive year, as [reported around the ceremony](https://m.allfootballapp.com/amp/news/Bundesliga/2026-Ballon-dOr-Awards-Roundup-Kane-claims-top-prize-Yamal-wins-third-straight-Kopa-Trophy/5734596). No other player has won it three times running since the award's introduction, and Barcelona's teenager is still young enough to be favourite again next year.

## 4. Rodri (Barcelona, Spain)

Rodri's year had two defining moments. First, he captained Spain to the World Cup, beating Argentina 1-0 after extra time in the final on 19 July 2026, Ferran Torres scoring the winner in the 106th minute at MetLife Stadium for Spain's second World Cup after 2010. Then, on 18 August, he completed a move away from the club he won it all with: [Barcelona confirmed his signing from Manchester City](https://www.news4jax.com/news/2026/08/18/barcelona-confirms-signing-of-spain-captain-rodri-from-manchester-city/), in a deal reported at around £65m (€76.5m) and running to June 2030.

## 5. Lionel Messi (Inter Miami, Argentina)

Messi's Argentina came up a goal short in New Jersey, losing the World Cup final 1-0 to Spain after extra time, and he was visibly emotional collecting his runners-up medal. It was still enough, alongside his club form for Inter Miami, to finish fifth in the Ballon d'Or voting at 39, the highest finish of anyone whose country did not win the tournament he was chasing.

## 6. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia (Paris Saint-Germain, Georgia)

Kvaratskhelia's 2025-26 Champions League was among the best in the competition: 16 combined goals and assists by a forward, tied with Kane and Mbappé for the most of anyone, according to [StatMuse's season tracker](https://www.statmuse.com/fc/ask/forward-with-the-most-goal-contribution-2026?l=ucl). PSG's run ended in a 4-3 penalty-shootout win over Arsenal in the final on 30 May 2026 in Budapest, their second Champions League in a row, and Kvaratskhelia's sixth-place finish is the highest of the three PSG players in this top ten.

## 7. Ousmane Dembélé (Paris Saint-Germain, France)

A year after winning the Ballon d'Or himself, Dembélé dropped to seventh but still outranked several players with bigger individual numbers. His biggest moment of the season came in the final he and PSG won: his 65th-minute penalty cancelled out Kai Havertz's early opener against Arsenal and forced extra time in a match PSG went on to win on penalties, retaining the Champions League they won the year before.

## 8. Erling Haaland (Manchester City, Norway)

Haaland's 2025-26 Champions League produced 8 combined goals and assists, among the competition's leading forwards that season per the same StatMuse tracker. Manchester City's season did not end in the trophies Haaland's numbers usually bring, and eighth in the Ballon d'Or is his lowest finish among the players on this list relative to his usual standard, behind three Paris Saint-Germain players and a teammate-turned-rival in Rodri.

## 9. Michael Olise (Bayern Munich, France)

Olise's partnership with Kane at Bayern Munich is central to his ninth-place finish: he scored twice as the club opened their 2026-27 Champions League campaign on matchday one, continuing the form that made him one of the Ballon d'Or's rising names. He is the second Bayern player in the top ten, alongside the winner himself.

## 10. Fabián Ruiz (Paris Saint-Germain, Spain)

Ruiz completes the top ten and the trio of Paris Saint-Germain players on it, having anchored the midfield that beat Arsenal on penalties to retain the Champions League. It is PSG's biggest single-club representation in this ranking, a direct result of becoming the first team since Real Madrid to win the competition back-to-back.
