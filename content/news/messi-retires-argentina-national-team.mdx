Lionel Messi has played his last match for Argentina. The 39-year-old announced his international retirement on Monday, 20 years after his first cap, closing a career of 207 matches, 125 goals and 68 assists in the blue and white.

He is not retiring from football. He continues at Inter Miami.

> "I leave with the peace of mind and pride of having given you, together with my teammates, moments we'd only dreamed of. It was incredible to have lived through it all with you."
>
> - Lionel Messi, in a social media post translated from Spanish

## The decision was written in July

Messi says he wrote the announcement two days after Argentina lost the World Cup final to Spain, then held it. The pause included mourning the death of his father. He says he came out of it more certain.

> "It was a decision that hurt, and still hurts, deep in the soul, but I understand that it's time. I swear to you that I always gave it my all, not just in recent years, but even before we won everything."
>
> - Lionel Messi

> "There's not much time left, and chapters are coming to a close, and this is one that hurts me deeply. I love, have loved, and will always love being on the national team. I've given it my all; I have nothing left to give, and besides, there are some truly great young players coming up who deserve their spot."
>
> - Lionel Messi

He signed off by putting himself back among the people he started with.

> "Thank you for all the love over these 20 years. Now I'll be just one of you, always cheering and supporting from the sidelines, through the good times and the bad, especially the bad."
>
> - Lionel Messi

## The years when it did not work

The international record is not the story most Argentines remember first. For a decade, Messi was the best player in the world at Barcelona and the man who kept losing finals with his country: three Copa America finals and the 2014 World Cup final.

The lowest point came in 2016. He missed his penalty in the shootout defeat to Chile and walked away that night.

> "For me, the national team is over. I've done all I can. It hurts not to be a champion. It was the thing I wanted the most but I couldn't get it, so I think it's over."
>
> - Lionel Messi, after the 2016 Copa America final

Weeks later he came back, and framed the reversal as a choice to fix things from inside rather than criticise from outside.

> "I see there are many problems in Argentinian football and I don't intend to create another one. We need to fix many things in Argentinian football, but I prefer to do this from inside and not criticise from outside."
>
> - Lionel Messi, 2016

Nothing changed immediately. Argentina went out in the round of 16 in 2018 and finished third at the 2019 Copa America. The comparisons to Diego Maradona, who wore the same number, coached him for his country and won the 1986 World Cup, were relentless, and Maradona joined in.

> "We shouldn't deify Messi any longer. He's Messi when he plays for Barcelona. Messi is Messi when he wears that shirt and he's another Messi with Argentina."
>
> - Diego Maradona, speaking to Fox Sports

## Then it all arrived at once

After the pandemic delays, the trophies came in a cluster: the 2021 Copa America, the 2022 World Cup in Qatar, and the 2024 Copa America.

This summer showed the scoring had not gone anywhere. Messi finished the tournament with eight goals and four assists, second only to Kylian Mbappe, and briefly became the all-time leading scorer in World Cup history with 21 before Mbappe overtook him at 22. It ended with [defeat to Spain in the final](/news/fifa-sanctions-world-cup-final-brawl), his sixth World Cup and his second final loss.

## Key facts at a glance

- 207 caps, 125 goals, 68 assists for Argentina across 20 years.
- Six World Cups: won 2022, runner-up in 2014 and 2026.
- Copa America winner in 2021 and 2024, after three final defeats.
- Eight goals and four assists at the 2026 World Cup, second only to Mbappe.
- Held the World Cup all-time scoring record at 21 goals until Mbappe reached 22.
- Continues to play club football for Inter Miami.
