Manchester City have signed 17-year-old winger Jeremy Monga from Leicester City for a fee of £10m plus add-ons, Sky Sports News understands. Monga, who turned 17 on Friday, has put pen to paper on a five-year contract running until the summer of 2031.

The move comes as a surprise twist in the transfer market. Monga had been expected to join Arsenal after holding talks with the Gunners, but City moved in late to complete the deal at a valuation Arsenal were unwilling to match.

## A rare talent at a young age

Monga is the third-youngest player to ever appear in the Premier League, behind Arsenal pair Ethan Nwaneri and Max Dowman. He made his top-flight debut against Newcastle United in April 2025 at just 15 years and 271 days old, making him Leicester's second-youngest ever appearance maker.

He was so young at the time of his debut that he was legally barred from wearing Leicester's front-of-shirt betting sponsor on his jersey.

- England U19 international.
- Made 37 senior appearances for Leicester in total, including 7 in the Premier League.
- Played 30 times across all competitions in 2025/26, scoring 1 goal and providing 2 assists.

Despite Leicester's relegation from the Premier League last season, top clubs continued to track Monga's progress closely.

## What Monga said

Speaking to Manchester City's official website, Monga said joining the club felt like an easy decision once he learned of their interest.

> "When I became aware that Manchester City were interested, I knew instantly this was the right choice for me. For any young footballer, to become part of this amazing club is a dream come true. This has been the best club in England over the past 10 years."
>
> - Jeremy Monga, Manchester City winger

He pointed to the club's track record of developing academy players into first-team regulars, citing Phil Foden and Nico O'Reilly as examples.

> "It is a privilege to be here, and I'm delighted to have joined."
>
> - Jeremy Monga, Manchester City winger

## Third English signing of the summer

Monga becomes the third English player to join new head coach Enzo Maresca's squad this summer. He follows goalkeeper Pierce Charles and record signing Elliot Anderson, who arrived for £116m.
