Luciano Spalletti has named a Juventus squad for Saturday evening's Serie A trip to Parma without four players: Kenan Yildiz, Weston McKennie, Khephren Thuram and Edon Zhegrova. Kick-off is 20:45 local time, 19:45 BST.

Three of the four were expected. Spalletti confirmed the absences of Yildiz and McKennie on the eve of the game, and Thuram was never likely to be involved.

## Zhegrova is the surprise

The Kosovo international had been expected to be fit enough for a place on the bench. He is not in the squad at all.

Il Bianconero puts it down to muscle fatigue. The timing is awkward: Sky Sport Italia reported on Friday that Lazio have approached Juventus about him, with [the transfer window](/guides/how-transfer-windows-work) closing on 1 September.

## The injury picture

- **Kenan Yildiz** - out for more than two months after surgery on a fractured foot.
- **Weston McKennie** - muscle problem.
- **Khephren Thuram** - knee problem, with a specialist consultation booked for next week. His absence may also run long.
- **Edon Zhegrova** - muscle fatigue.

Set against that, Jeff Ekhator is back. The Italy international returns to a squad that opened the Serie A season with a win away at Frosinone last week.

## Juventus squad list for Parma

**Goalkeepers:** Grabara, Pinsoglio, Vicario

**Defenders:** Celik, Bremer, Gatti, Kalulu, Cambiaso, Rugani, Lucumi

**Midfielders:** Locatelli, Koopmeiners, Douglas Luiz

**Attackers:** Conceicao, Kolo Muani, Boga, Milik, Alajbegovic, Ekhator, David, Nico Gonzalez

Three fit senior midfielders is the number that stands out. With Thuram and McKennie both out, Spalletti's options in the middle are down to Locatelli, Koopmeiners and Douglas Luiz, which is likely to mean an attacker dropping in.

## Key facts at a glance

- Parma vs Juventus kicks off at 20:45 local time, 19:45 BST.
- Yildiz, McKennie, Thuram and Zhegrova are all out.
- Yildiz faces more than two months out following foot surgery.
- Thuram sees a specialist next week about his knee.
- Ekhator returns; Juventus beat Frosinone away in their opening fixture.
