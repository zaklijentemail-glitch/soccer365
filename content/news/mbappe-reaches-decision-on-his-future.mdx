Kylian Mbappe has reportedly made his decision on where he will play next season, with sources close to the player suggesting an official announcement is imminent.

## Why the uncertainty

The forward's contract situation has been the subject of speculation for months, with several of Europe's biggest clubs said to be monitoring developments closely. That uncertainty has fuelled near-constant transfer talk across the French and Spanish press, each citing different sources close to the player's camp.

## What happens now

With the decision reportedly made, attention turns to the formal announcement, which is expected to follow standard steps: medical checks, contract signing, and an official unveiling by the club. Until a club confirms the move, reports of Mbappe's decision remain unofficial, though the growing consensus among outlets suggests a resolution is close.
