Jurgen Klopp is returning to management, with multiple reports confirming he has agreed a deal to take charge of the Germany national team.

The 58-year-old has been out of coaching since leaving Liverpool, and his appointment ends months of speculation over who would replace the outgoing head coach.

Klopp is expected to be officially unveiled this week, with his first match in charge coming next month.
