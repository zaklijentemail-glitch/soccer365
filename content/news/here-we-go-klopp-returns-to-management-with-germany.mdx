Jurgen Klopp is returning to management, with multiple reports confirming he has agreed a deal to take charge of the Germany national team. It brings an end to his time away from the touchline and hands the national side one of the most sought-after coaches in world football.

## Why this appointment stands out

The 58-year-old built his reputation on high-intensity, attacking football at Borussia Dortmund and Liverpool, winning major trophies at both clubs before stepping away from the game. His return, this time in charge of his home nation, is seen as a major coup for the German federation as they look to rebuild after a difficult recent tournament cycle.

## What comes next

Klopp is expected to be officially unveiled this week, with a first press conference likely to address his plans for the squad and coaching staff. His first match in charge is scheduled for next month, giving him a short window to assess the group before competitive fixtures resume.
