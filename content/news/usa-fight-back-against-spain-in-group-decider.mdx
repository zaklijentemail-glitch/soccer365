The United States fought back from a goal down to draw 2-2 with Spain in a thrilling final group game, a result that sends both teams through to the knockout stage.

## How the comeback unfolded

Spain took the lead and looked in control for large parts of the first half, but the USA responded with two second-half goals to level the score before a late Spanish equaliser made it 2-2. The result was enough for both sides to secure their place in the last 16, sparing either team the anxious wait for other results to go their way.

## Why the result matters

The comeback will give the USA plenty of confidence heading into the knockout stage, having shown they can fight back against one of the tournament favourites rather than folding once behind. For Spain, the dropped points are a minor blemish on an otherwise strong group campaign, though it means they now face a tougher run through the bracket depending on how the group finishes settle.

## What's next

Both teams now turn their attention to the round of 16, where they are likely to face a tough test against sides who topped their own groups.
