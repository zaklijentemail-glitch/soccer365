The sky over Old Trafford turned dark grey in the closing minutes, thunder arrived, and the rain came through a leak in the roof on cue. It would have made a neat metaphor for a miserable afternoon.

It was not a miserable afternoon. Manchester United won 5-2, and most of the reason wears the armband.

Marcus Rashford made his first Premier League start for the club since December 2024. Kobbie Mainoo was in Michael Carrick's line-up too. Neither was the story by the end.

> "What else can I say? We've said quite a lot about him already, and I've only been here six or seven months."
>
> - Michael Carrick, Manchester United head coach

## A week that turned around

The season opened badly for Bruno Fernandes, who was among the many United players well below the required level in the [2-0 defeat at Hull City](/news/premier-league-questions-arsenal-manchester-united).

Tuesday was better. He collected the PFA Players' Player of the Year award for the season that took United back into the Champions League after two years away, one in which he registered a Premier League record 21 assists.

Before kick-off against Ipswich he walked out with the sons of PC Matthew Blades, killed in a head-on collision on the A66 near Middlesbrough.

## Anonymous, then decisive

For long stretches he was barely involved, and United fell behind to Gary O'Neil's side. Then the openings came.

- **1-1:** Marcelino Nunez slipped under no pressure, Matheus Cunha ran clear and squared for his captain, who finished emphatically.
- **3-1:** a penalty in front of the Stretford End on the hour, struck without hesitation. Worth remembering he had missed two spot-kicks in pre-season.
- **The hat-trick:** seven minutes later, his third for the club.

He nearly produced something that would have overshadowed all of it. Ipswich gave the ball away straight from a restart and Fernandes went for goal from 55 yards. Kjell Scherpen backpedalled in a hurry and was grateful the ball finished on the roof of his net.

> "He's the captain. He's been here for a good number of years now and experienced a whole load of different emotions. Times like today, when you're looking towards certain players to give us that little bit of something else, he did particularly well."
>
> - Michael Carrick, Manchester United head coach

## The contract question nobody at United wants

Fernandes is in the final year of his deal, with a club option for a further year. Interest from elsewhere in Europe, Galatasaray included, will not produce a departure this month.

There is still an awkward fact sitting underneath it. The reigning Players' Player of the Year is not the club's highest earner. Rashford is. At 31, Fernandes could reasonably point at that.

> "He is the one that gets Manchester United ticking. There is a reason why he was voted player of the year by his fellow professionals and it's performances like this. He wants to be the best and he wants to win. If they don't have him, there is going to be a huge hole in this team."
>
> - Ashley Young, former Manchester United and England winger, speaking to Sky Sports

## The detail that explains him

At full-time his first move was not to celebrate. It was to find substitute Benjamin Sesko and talk through a pass he had been trying to play into him in stoppage time.

He then shook hands with the Ipswich players and the officials, and was setting off to greet more opponents when he remembered the match ball. He turned back, asked, and Craig Pawson handed it over.

> "There are certain days you feel it. It is one of those days when everything comes to you and you have to take it. I hate losing and it is difficult to be positive when you lose games. It is very difficult for me to control the emotions. At this club you want to be on top of everything."
>
> - Bruno Fernandes, speaking to BBC Sport

Without him United probably do not win this game. That sentence has covered a lot of matches over the past two years, which is the part Carrick has to solve.

## Key facts at a glance

- Manchester United 5-2 Ipswich Town at Old Trafford, after falling behind.
- Fernandes scored three, his third United hat-trick, including a penalty at the Stretford End.
- He was named PFA Players' Player of the Year the previous Tuesday.
- He set a Premier League record with 21 assists last season.
- Rashford started his first league game for the club since December 2024; Mainoo also started.
- Fernandes is in the final year of his contract, with a one-year club option.
