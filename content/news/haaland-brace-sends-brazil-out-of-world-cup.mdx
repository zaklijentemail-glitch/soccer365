Norway have reached the World Cup quarter-finals for the first time in the country's history, beating five-time champions Brazil 2-1 thanks to a second-half brace from Erling Haaland.

Brazil dominated large spells of possession but could not find a way past a well-organised Norwegian defence, and were made to pay when Haaland struck twice in the space of eleven minutes.

The result ends Brazil's tournament at the last-16 stage and sets up a quarter-final meeting for Norway against the winner of the other second-round tie.
